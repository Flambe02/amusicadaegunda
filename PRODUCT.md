# Product

## Platform

web

One React/Vite codebase serves every surface: the responsive web SPA and PWA, the Capacitor Android (and iOS) wrapper, and a dedicated 10-foot Android TV / Google TV interface driven by D-pad. The wrapper does not make the design language native. The TV layout is a web layout built for distance viewing and remote-control focus.

## Users

**Primary:** Brazilian adults who follow the news and come for the humour. Each Monday they check what this week's story has turned into, listen to or watch the new parody, and then may wander into the catalogue, the lyrics or karaoke.

**Secondary audiences (confirmed, not primary):**
- Families and friends singing together in the same room: karaoke and Festa on the TV, with phones as optional companions.
- French speakers learning Brazilian Portuguese (A1–B1) through the songs in Modo Aprender (`/apprender`).
- The product owner and admins, who use the Back Office to publish songs, edit lyrics and sync karaoke by hand.

## Product Purpose

A Música da Segunda is an independent Brazilian project that turns the week's news into an original satirical song, released every Monday. The site publishes each new song, keeps every past song in a browsable archive, and links out to the platforms where the songs live (YouTube, Shorts, Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok, Instagram).

Success means that the weekly release is the reason people come back, and that the archive, karaoke, Festa and Aprender give them reasons to stay. For the interactive modes, the V3 North Star applies: a user opens the app, understands it, finds a song and starts singing in under 30 seconds, with no account, no paywall and no microphone.

## Positioning

**The weekly news-to-song ritual.** Every Monday, a real story from Brazil's week becomes an original song. Taken together, the songs form a chronicle of Brazil set to music, and the catalogue is its archive. A generic parody channel or karaoke app can't honestly claim this, because it depends on a steady weekly habit of topical songwriting done in-house.

Karaoke, Festa, Aprender and the Roda (random wheel) are ways to spend time with that catalogue. They are not the identity.

## Operating Context

- **Monday release:** a new song appears. The homepage should always show the latest release, and it must never tell a visitor that nothing has been published.
- **Discovery:** people arrive from Google, social shares (WhatsApp previews in particular) and the platforms. Individual song pages at `/musica/:slug/` are the main search entry points.
- **Living-room use:** karaoke and Festa on a TV seen from a distance. Phones join by QR code or short code plus a nickname and can add songs to the queue and react.
- **Study use:** the reading, karaoke and vocabulary-notebook flows in Modo Aprender.
- **Admin use:** a Back Office connected to Supabase where karaoke timing is synced line by line by hand.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack:** React 18 + Vite, Tailwind + Radix UI, Lucide icons. Supabase handles data, Auth (admins only), Realtime and Edge Functions. The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages from `docs/`. Crawlable static HTML stubs are generated by `scripts/generate-stubs.cjs`.
- **SEO is load-bearing.** Every SEO fix has to be made in two places: the static stubs and the React components. Any new surface must not cause the stub SEO output to regress.
- **Architecture stays simple** (a V3 decision): direct browser-to-Supabase access under RLS. No application server, no generic API layer.
- **Open and frictionless** (a V3 decision): no public accounts, no sign-in, no paywall and no monetization in the current phases.
- **Festa is a simple family experience:** the TV controls playback and the queue, and phones add songs and react. Users never see any security concepts.
- **Karaoke sync is 100% manual.** Automatic sync was attempted and removed. Public visibility of a song's karaoke depends on `isKaraokePublished()`.
- **Schema changes are additive only.** The Supabase column names are misleading: `youtube_music_url` holds the Short (the main embed) and `youtube_url` holds the YouTube Music link.
- **UI language:** Brazilian Portuguese. Aprender addresses French speakers.
- **Future directions, documented but not built:** Family/Kids content, Fire TV and Samsung Tizen, Creator Studio, accounts and monetization.

Never write the catalogue size into code or copy: compute it. Each song has lyrics, links to its platforms, and synced karaoke on some songs.

## Product Principles

1. **The Monday song comes first.** Every surface should make it obvious what this week's song is and what news it came from.
2. **The archive is a chronicle.** Past songs are a record of Brazil's recent history, not filler, so the catalogue should never look empty or stale.
3. **From open to singing in 30 seconds.** No accounts, no paywalls, no setup rituals.
4. **The TV is the stage and the phone is optional.** The living-room modes must work fully from the remote.
5. **Degrade gracefully and never show an empty state.** Most songs lack the optional editorial fields, so no surface should render an empty block, a placeholder or "não disponível".

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- The TV interface must be usable entirely by D-pad focus, with visible focus states, and readable from across a room.
- Karaoke must work without a microphone.
- Aprender is aimed at A1–B1 learners, so its language support should be scaffolded for that level.
